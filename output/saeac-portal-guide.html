04 Judge & Change Maker

Judge — judge@test.com

https://senatorquiz.vercel.app/portal/match

The judge sees only the assigned Grand Finale match.

  • Striker answer: +1 point
  • Assist answer: +0.5 points
  • VAR referral: 0 points
  • Coach substitutions

Seeded score: 2.5 versus 1.0. Events are append-only; corrections use logged adjustments.

Change Maker — volunteer@test.com

https://senatorquiz.vercel.app/portal/volunteer

The account is accepted and assigned to the Grand Finale Check-in shift at the Registration desk. Two volunteer briefings are available.

07 Important notes

  • The database contains two schools, one approved school, one pending school, a complete five-student roster, two judges, an accepted volunteer, a live match, scores, nine accreditation badges, venues, fixtures, and a news article.
  • Public pages display approved information only. Pending schools and student records remain private.
  • Security is enforced in Postgres, not only in the interface. For example, school@test.com should be redirected away from the admin portal.
  • /schedule and /news may be cached for up to one hour. /live and authenticated portal pages update immediately.
