# SAEAC news editor

1. Sign in to the portal and open **News management**.
2. To write a new update, complete the article form and save it as a draft.
3. Find the article, open **Edit article**, and change the title, summary, category or body. Select **Save changes**.
4. Use **Preview saved article** to see a draft privately. The preview shows only saved content.
5. To add an image, save the article first. Under **Upload an image**, select a JPEG, PNG or WebP file below 3 MB. Choose **Replace cover** or **Add to article gallery**. Gallery images need a short caption.
6. To add a YouTube video, enter its link and a short title and select **Add video**. Visitors choose whether to play it.
7. Check the preview, then change the article status to **published**. Draft and review articles remain private.

Gallery images and videos appear at the end of the saved article. In the body editor they are represented by lines such as `[[image:/api/news-media/...|Caption]]`. You can move an entire line to change its position or remove it from the article; keep a blank line above and below it. Avoid changing the image path itself.

Save any text edits before uploading media. If somebody else changes the article while you are editing, copy your unsaved text and reload before trying again. An image added to an already published article is visible to the public once saved. Archiving an article stops new access to its private uploaded images, but anyone who already downloaded an image retains their copy.
